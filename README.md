<h2>:zap:Electrical Transformers Manager App</h2>
Desktop application aimed at improving the management and real-time tracking of transformers for distribution departments at national electricity and gas company (Sonelgaz). The project focused on providing a solution for the oversight of transformer inventory, monitoring their status, and managing their movements between different locations.Helping streamline operations, improve data accuracy, and facilitate collaboration between departments by offering a centralized, secure platform for transformer management.
<h2>:hammer_and_wrench:Key technologies</h2>
- Compose multiplatform </br>
- Ktor
